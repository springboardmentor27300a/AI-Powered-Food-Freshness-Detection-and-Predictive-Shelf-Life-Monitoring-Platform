01 · Objective

Reduce food waste with intelligent freshness monitoring

Mission

Build an AI-powered Food Freshness Monitoring Platform that uses image analysis, environmental conditions and storage information to estimate food freshness, predict remaining shelf life, detect spoilage indicators and generate storage recommendations.

The platform helps consumers, retailers, restaurants, warehouses, food manufacturers and supply chain operators reduce food waste and improve food quality.

Outcomes

  • AI-powered freshness platform developed and deployed
  • Secure authentication & role-based access control
  • Image-based food freshness assessment workflows
  • Shelf-life prediction & spoilage detection systems
  • Storage condition monitoring & optimization modules
  • Freshness scoring & food quality analytics
  • Dashboards for freshness monitoring & inventory insights
  • Deployed with Docker on cloud platforms such as AWS or Azure
