Personalized Food Storage Tips

🍎
Fresh Fruits & Vegetables (Crisper Drawer)

Keep apples, bananas, and avocados separate from leafy greens to avoid accelerated ethylene ripening. Store greens in perforated bags at 85-95% RH.

🥛
Dairy & Plant Milks (Internal Shelf)

Store milk, cream, and yogurts on middle refrigerator shelves (2–4°C) rather than door compartments, where frequent door openings cause temperature swings.

🍞
Bakery Goods & Artisan Breads

Never refrigerate fresh artisan bread—retrogradation accelerates staling at 4°C. Keep at room temperature in a bread box or slice and freeze immediately.

🥩
Raw Meat & Seafood (Bottom Shelf)

Maintain at 0–2°C on bottom refrigerator shelf in leak-proof trays. Consume within 48 hours or vacuum-seal and store in the freezer (-18°C).

Ethylene Gas Management & Segregation Rule

Ethylene is an odorless, naturally occurring plant hormone that triggers ripening and rapid senescence. High Ethylene Producers: Apples, Bananas, Avocados, Melons, and Pears. Ethylene-Sensitive Foods: Leafy greens, Broccoli, Cucumbers, Carrots, and Herbs. Rule of Thumb: Always segregate producers in a dedicated fruit bowl or separate refrigerator bin to avoid premature yellowing and rotting of sensitive greens.
