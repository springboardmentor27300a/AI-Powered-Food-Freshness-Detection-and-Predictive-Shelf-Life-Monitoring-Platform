🔬 How FreshSense AI Calculates Shelf-Life

Our estimation combines empirical food microbiology baselines with kinetic Arrhenius reaction rates:

1. Category Baseline

Uses calibrated baseline lifespans under optimal refrigeration (e.g., Seafood: 2 days, Meat: 3 days, Dairy: 10 days, Fruits: 7 days).

2. Visual Freshness Factor

Multiplies the remaining lifespan by the AI freshness score percentage. Samples showing degradation experience proportional lifespan deductions.

3. Q10 Temperature Penalty

Biochemical degradation reaction rates double for every 10°C elevation above optimal refrigeration threshold according to Q10 kinetics.
