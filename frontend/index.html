How the AI Food Freshness Engine Operates

From image capture to prescriptive cold-chain storage: a verified 5-stage automated pipeline.

1
Upload Food Image
Ingest photo via camera, file drag-and-drop, or web address URL for non-invasive multi-spectral inspection.
2
AI Freshness Analysis
OpenCV extracts HSV chrominance, surface texture variance, and oxidation ratios to detect early spoilage.
3
Quality Score
Calculates standardized 0–100% Quality Score and classification (Fresh, Moderately Fresh, or Spoiled).
4
Shelf-Life Prediction
Applies Arrhenius reaction kinetics to forecast remaining safe consumption hours and days under ambient vs chilled storage.
5
Storage Recommendations
Generates prescriptive recommendations for temperature adjustment, crisper ventilation, and FEFO inventory dispatch.
